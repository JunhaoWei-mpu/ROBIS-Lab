Robotics and Intelligent Systems Lab

ROBIS Lab studies autonomous robots and intelligent computational methods. Our research covers UAV navigation and mission planning, robotic manipulation, vision–language–action systems, optimization, and UAV communications.

We work on perception, planning, and decision-making, using aerial and mobile robotic platforms to study these methods in physical systems.
