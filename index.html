Robotics and Intelligent Systems Lab

The Robotics and Intelligent Systems Lab (ROBIS) at Macao Polytechnic University studies autonomous robots and intelligent computational methods. We investigate how robots perceive their surroundings, plan safe and purposeful motion, and act on visual observations and human instructions.

Our work connects algorithm development with physical systems, including autonomous UAVs, mobile dual-arm robots, and UAV–manipulator platforms. These systems support research in navigation, coordination, physical interaction, and demonstration data collection.

  • Autonomous Aerial Robotics — perception, navigation, trajectory planning, and aerial mission generation.
  • Robotic Manipulation and Mobile Robots — robot arms, dual-arm coordination, mobile manipulation, and UAV–manipulator integration.
  • Vision–Language–Action and Embodied Intelligence — visual understanding, language-guided task planning, and robot execution.
  • Intelligent Computing and Optimization — metaheuristic algorithms, reinforcement learning, multi-agent decisions, and knowledge representation.
  • UAV Communications and Networked Systems — wireless signal prediction, satellite/NR5G signal processing, and communication-aware UAV planning.
