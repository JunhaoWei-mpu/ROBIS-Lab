Our research

Autonomy, grounded
in physical systems.

ROBIS Lab — the Robotics and Intelligent Systems Lab — studies autonomous robots and intelligent computational methods. Our research spans aerial robotics, robotic manipulation, vision–language–action systems, and learning and optimization.

We connect perception, planning, and decision-making with physical robotic platforms to explore how intelligent systems can operate in the real world.
