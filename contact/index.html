Work with ROBIS Lab

We welcome enquiries from students, researchers, and engineers interested in autonomous robots and intelligent systems. Our work brings together aerial autonomy, robotic manipulation, embodied intelligence, optimization, and UAV communications.

To discuss a research idea or a potential opportunity, please write to Yapeng Wang with a brief introduction and the topic you would like to explore.

Prospective students

If you are interested in master's or doctoral research related to the lab, please introduce your academic background and the questions you would like to investigate.

A useful first email includes:

  • Your current degree and academic background.
  • The research direction that interests you and why.
  • A brief CV and links to relevant projects, code, or publications, if available.

Research collaboration

We are interested in discussing shared research problems in UAV navigation and mission planning, robotic manipulation, learning and optimization, and communication-aware autonomous systems.
