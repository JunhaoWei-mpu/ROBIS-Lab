Erli Lyu is a Lecturer in the Faculty of Applied Sciences at Macao Polytechnic University. He received his PhD in Mechanical Engineering from Harbin Institute of Technology (Shenzhen) in 2023, an MSc in Control Science and Engineering from the same institution in 2016, and a bachelor’s degree in Measurement and Control Technology and Instrumentation from Northeastern University at Qinhuangdao in 2014. His research focuses on neural motion planning, point cloud representation learning, autonomous navigation in densely populated environments, reinforcement learning, and human–robot cooperation.
