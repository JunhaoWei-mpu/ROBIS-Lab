Liang Yu received his bachelor's degree in Computer Science and Technology from Jianghuai College of Anhui University and is currently pursuing a master's degree in Big Data and Internet of Things at Macao Polytechnic University. His research interests focus on applying large language models and large-scale vision models to UAV systems, particularly for visual perception, autonomous navigation, and mission planning.
