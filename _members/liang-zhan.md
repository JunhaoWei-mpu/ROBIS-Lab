Liang Zhan received his bachelor's degree in Information Management and Information Systems from Guangzhou Huali University. His graduate studies at Macao Polytechnic University focus on Big Data and Internet of Things. His research interests include drone visual perception and multimodal understanding, with a focus on aerial visual referential localization, spatial relationship inference, and compositional generalization.
