Junhao Wei received his bachelor’s degree in Unmanned Aerial Vehicle System Engineering from the School of Aeronautics at Beijing Institute of Technology, Zhuhai, in 2023, and his master’s degree in Big Data and Internet of Things from Macao Polytechnic University in June 2025. During his master’s studies, he worked as a Research Assistant, focusing on drone trajectory generation. Since September 2025, he has been pursuing a PhD in Computer Applied Technology at Macao Polytechnic University. His research interests include aerial robotics, motion planning, and autonomous mission generation.
