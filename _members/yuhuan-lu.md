Yuhuan Lu is an Assistant Professor at Macao Polytechnic University. He received his PhD in Computer Science from the University of Macau in 2025, and his bachelor’s and master’s degrees from Sun Yat-sen University in 2017 and 2020, respectively. His research connects artificial intelligence and knowledge representation with transportation and healthcare. His interests include deep learning, reinforcement learning, and generative AI for prediction, recognition, decision-making, planning, and human–machine interaction.
