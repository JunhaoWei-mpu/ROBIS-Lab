Haochen Li received his Bachelor of Engineering in Robotics from the School of Industrial Automation at Beijing Institute of Technology, Zhuhai, in 2025. In August 2025, he began his master’s studies in Big Data and Internet of Things at Macao Polytechnic University. His research focuses on robotic systems and trajectory planning algorithms for robotic manipulators.
