Long Liu received the B.E. degree in Software Engineering from Shanxi Agricultural University in 2020. He is currently pursuing the M.S. degree in Big Data and Internet of Things with Macao Polytechnic University. During his graduate studies, he has participated in multiple projects related to UAV wireless communication and path planning. His research interests include wireless communications, UAV signal prediction, machine learning, and AI applications.
