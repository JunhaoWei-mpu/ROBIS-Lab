Xuan Cheng received the B.E. degree in Industrial Engineering from Xi’an Technological University in 2015 and the M.E. degree in Industrial Engineering from Northwestern Polytechnical University in 2020. From 2020 to 2025, he worked as a Senior Engineer in terminal protocol development at ZTE Corporation. He is currently pursuing the Ph.D. degree in Computer Application Technology at Macao Polytechnic University. His main research interests include the integration of low-orbit satellite/NR5G signal processing with artificial intelligence and AI-driven path planning for multi-UAV systems.
